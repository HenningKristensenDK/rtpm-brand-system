RTPM Brand System v4 · Step 2 of 4 · High-frequency marketing assets

LinkedIn & marketing graphics

Templates at true export dimensions, shown at 40%. Marketing runs the dark, bold end of the system — the same tokens as the product, turned up. Every canvas is measured in real pixels so type sizes read straight off the spec. All copy, numbers and subject matter are placeholder: what is under review here is the template system and the visual hierarchy, not the content.

One highlight, not one word
One text highlight per canvas in #FFCC00: a single contiguous span of one to four words forming one grammatical unit, on dark only. Two separated text highlights is the violation, not two words. A yellow tag counts as it — then the headline stays white. Structural gold is separate and not a text highlight: one divider rule, step marker or swipe cue per canvas is permitted alongside it, because a boundary or a marker does not compete with a word. On light grounds the highlight is Indigo #0D08D2; never both colours on one canvas. If a canvas needs two highlights to communicate, the message is not clear enough yet — see RTPM 1, section 10.
Sentence case, 5–7 words
Barlow Semi Condensed Bold, line-height 1.1. No Title Case, no em-dashes in headlines, no question marks stacked on hooks.
Logo is rationed
No logo on face-and-hook or carousel interiors. Permitted on the closer, milestone cards and banners. Placed artwork only, never re-typed.
Placeholder until approved
Every canvas ships with placeholder copy and placeholder figures. Nothing is populated until RTPM approves the case-study numbers and subject matter.
Format doctrine — two formats, two designs
A 16:9 asset is never a cropped 4:5. Each format is composed for where it is read.
Candidate for locking
4:5 Mobile-first 1080 × 1350
Read at 400px on a phone. Minimum headline 88px, minimum body 25px. Nothing below 24px survives.
Vertical stacking. One column, information descending. Never side-by-side columns — they halve to unreadable.
Bottom 180px is at risk. The caption and the swipe cue crowd it. Nothing essential below 1170px.
Thumb-scale test. The asset must land its point at 25% before it earns the tap.
16:9 Presentation-first 1920 × 1080
Three destinations. Slide deck, video thumbnail, website hero. All three are viewed on a wide screen at distance.
Horizontal division. Content sits in two or three vertical zones. The width is the asset — filling it with one centred headline wastes the format.
Minimum headline 96px. Body 26px. Thumbnails go further: 132px minimum, four words maximum.
104px margins. Wider than 4:5 so the composition breathes on a projected screen.
✓Do
Rewrite the headline for each format. Four words in 16:9 is a different sentence, not a shorter one.
Reposition the subject. A figure centred for 4:5 belongs at a third in 16:9.
Treat the two as siblings sharing tokens, not one asset in two sizes.
×Don't
Export 16:9 by cropping the top and bottom off a 4:5. Margins and type scale both break.
Letterbox a 4:5 into a 16:9 frame with navy bars.
Carry 4:5 type sizes into 16:9. They read small at presentation distance.

Linear strip — 16:9
1920 × 1080 · shown at 40% · max five steps
Candidate for locking
Purpose
Show an operational loop end to end in one wide image — the sequence a buyer recognises from their own project. This component is reused directly as the hero workflow sequence on rtpm.dk (see RTPM 4. Website Architecture, homepage above the fold).
Must include
Max five numbered steps and a takeaway title — a claim, not a topic label. "Early warning to live report in one day", not "Our workflow".
Layout rules
Steps on one baseline, equal width, connected by a 3px hairline. Step numbers in indigo circles on light. Body 26px, step label 38px. Owner or elapsed time under each step where it makes the point.

The 4:5 stacks number over statement. Here they sit side by side, because the width allows the eye to take both in one movement. The number gets its own left zone with a rule dividing it from the argument.

Candidate
Content gates. Single-stat (both formats), carousel data interior and framework matrix are locked as patterns only. They stay unpopulated until RTPM approves the figures and the subject matter. No number on any canvas in this document is a claim.
Three things I need from you
01 · Judge the pattern
Every figure and every subject on these canvases is placeholder. What is being reviewed is the grid, the hierarchy and the type scale. Approve those and the templates can be populated later without reopening the design.
02 · Content gates
Three templates cannot be published until content is approved: single-stat card, carousel data interior, and the framework matrix. They are locked as patterns, unpopulated as assets.
03 · A landscape portrait
The 16:9 formats are composed independently, not cropped from 4:5. That needs a wide-frame photograph with shoulder room on one side — the current cut-out is a portrait crop.
